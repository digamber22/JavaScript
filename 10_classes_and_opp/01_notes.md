## 4 pillars  (HomeWork) 
###  1. Abstraction 
👉 Hiding complex details and showing only essential features.

Example: A car has an engine, but you only use the pedals and steering wheel (you don’t need to know internal mechanics).

### 2. Encapsulation 
👉 Bundling data and methods that operate on that data into a single unit (class), and restricting direct access.

Example: An object has private properties (balance) and exposes public methods (getBalance()) to access them.

### 3. Inheritance 
 👉Creating new classes from existing classes, reusing their properties and methods.

Example: A Dog inherits characteristics from an Animal (both can eat and sleep).

### 4. Polymorphism 
👉 Same method name, but different behavior based on the object calling it.

Example: draw() method behaves differently for Circle, Rectangle, and Triangle classes.
